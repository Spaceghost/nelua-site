Theseus browser demo

QEMU Wasm 8.2.0 and the PC firmware package are from this upstream build:
https://github.com/ktock/qemu-wasm-demo-images/tree/b7c549b5e6f4c376f76483a03e983214421434ad/alpine-x86_64

Source at the revision recorded by the upstream build:
https://github.com/ktock/qemu-wasm/tree/8604ed49a3cde392890b014a8d5a959c8a2fe72a
https://github.com/ktock/qemu-wasm/tree/8604ed49a3cde392890b014a8d5a959c8a2fe72a/roms
https://github.com/ktock/qemu-wasm-demo/blob/0208c86ea45253c26c0ea6907f6db2dec89eb7b2/create-images.sh

QEMU and its firmware contain GPL and LGPL components as described in their
source trees. License texts are in COPYING-GPL-2.txt and COPYING-LGPL-2.1.txt.
The WebAssembly binary is wrapped in gzip for delivery, without changes to
the executable or its Emscripten loader and worker.

The Linux kernel is Alpine linux-lts 6.18.54-r0 for x86_64 (GPL-2.0-only).
The recipe, patches, configuration, and upstream source URL are here:
https://gitlab.alpinelinux.org/alpine/aports/-/tree/a4209606081414a674f0c0985ea9d362f1261720/main/linux-lts

The initramfs contains the Theseus-enabled Nebox build and minimal boot paths.
https://github.com/Spaceghost/nebox/commit/4dd2757a0faf6141f2981c99b4cbbe1d59643a2f

The terminal bundle uses xterm.js and its fit addon:
https://github.com/xtermjs/xterm.js
